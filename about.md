<div class="about-intro">
  <p class="eyebrow">About the builder</p>
  <h1>Research that stays close to the machine.</h1>
  <p class="lede">I am Yuhao Xu, an independent AI researcher and open-source systems builder based in China. I use the name HsuYugo online. NeuralEngine is the core project: a systems investigation into how generated programs can become inspectable behavior in a world.</p>
</div>

<div class="about-grid">
  <div>
    <h2>Current questions</h2>
    <p>How should a learning system represent memory after its transient state is gone? What does it take for a distributed training system to remain auditable when workers disappear? Can a generated game world be checked at the source and runtime levels? How can a camera, controller, and mechanism close the loop in a physical logistics task?</p>
    <p>Those questions connect the projects on this site. They are deliberately broader than one model family, but concrete enough to become a protocol, a repository, and a result.</p>
